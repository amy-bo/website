We need biotechnologists, chefs, coders, creatives, engineers, enthusiasts, environmentalists, makers, medics, microbiologists, nutritionists and others. Most of what needs doing right now doesn't need a lab coat.

## Ways to help

- **Join the conversation** on the [AMYBO forum](https://forum.amybo.org/). Reading along and asking questions counts.
- **Run or replicate an experiment** and [share what happened](/experiments/submit/), including when it doesn't work. The [home enrichment protocol](/projects/methods/) is written for exactly this.
- **Build or improve a part.** Most designs are on [GitHub](https://github.com/amy-bo/electroPioreactor) as OpenSCAD source. If you have a 3D printer and an opinion, we'd like both.
- **Improve this website.** Most pages have an "Edit page" link at the bottom. See the [contribution guidelines](/collaborate/contribute/).
- **Come along** to our next [event](/events/). The next one is in London on 13 November 2026.
- **Support us financially:** see [Fund us](/collaborate/fund_us/).
- **Tell us what you can offer** through our [contact form](https://contact.andeye.com/?source=amybo.org&subject=Volunteering%20with%20AMYBO&cb=Volunteering&cb=Running%20an%20experiment&cb=Building%20an%20electroPioreactor&cb=Funding%20or%20sponsorship&cb=Press%20or%20speaking&cb=Something%20else). Feel free to get involved in any way you like.
