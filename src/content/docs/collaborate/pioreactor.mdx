The [Pioreactor](https://pioreactor.com) is an affordable, open source bioreactor built around a Raspberry Pi. When we [surveyed the options in 2023](/projects/past/equipment/bioreactors/) it was the one on our shortlist you could actually buy with a sensible lead time, it was clearly under active development, and its founder answered our questions. It has been our main research tool ever since, and most of what we've built is an extension of it.

- The [electroPioreactor](/projects/electropioreactor/) adds electrolysis so hydrogen-oxidising bacteria can grow on hydrogen made in the vial. Its [plugin](https://github.com/amy-bo/electroPioreactor/tree/main/AEP-Plugin) runs it from the Pioreactor's own web interface.
- [PioFlo](/projects/pioflo/) turns a Pioreactor into a flow-through cell, so its software can control a fermenter of any size.
- [OpenValve](/projects/other-hardware/openvalve/) is an affordable open source pinch valve for the tubing.

Our fixes to Pioreactor's software go back upstream through our [fork](https://github.com/amy-bo/pioreactor). Kits come from [LabCrafter](https://labcrafter.co.uk) in the UK and [Pioreactor](https://pioreactor.com) elsewhere. See also the [experiments](/projects/past/experiments/) we ran on the first units, including the [control test](/projects/past/experiments/control-testing/) to check that two Pioreactors agree with each other.
