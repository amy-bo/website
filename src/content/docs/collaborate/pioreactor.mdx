The [Pioreactor](https://pioreactor.com) is an affordable, open source bioreactor built around a Raspberry Pi. It is our main community research tool.

We extend it for protein fermentation research:

- The [electroPioreactor](/docs/electropioreactor/) adds electrolysis so hydrogen-oxidising bacteria can grow on hydrogen made in the vial.
- [Pioflo](/docs/pioflo/) lets the Pioreactor software control flow-through fermenters of any size.
- [OpenValve](/docs/openvalve/) is an affordable open source pinch valve.

See also our notes on [bioreactors](/docs/equipment/bioreactors/) and the [experiments](/docs/experiments/) we have run.
