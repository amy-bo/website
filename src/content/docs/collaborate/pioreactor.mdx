The [Pioreactor](https://pioreactor.com) is an affordable, open source bioreactor built around a Raspberry Pi. It is our main community research tool.

We extend it for protein fermentation research:

- The [electroPioreactor](/projects/electropioreactor/) adds electrolysis so hydrogen-oxidising bacteria can grow on hydrogen made in the vial. Its [plugin](https://github.com/amy-bo/pioreactor-electroPioreactor-plugin) runs it from the Pioreactor web interface.
- [Pioflo](/projects/pioflo/) lets the Pioreactor software control flow-through fermenters of any size.
- [OpenValve](/projects/other-hardware/openvalve/) is an affordable open source pinch valve.

See also our notes on [bioreactors](/projects/past/equipment/bioreactors/) and the [experiments](/projects/past/experiments/) we have run.
