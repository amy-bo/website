AMYBO is a not-for-profit, and our work is funded by grants and supporters. Funding lets us build and share open hardware, run experiments and keep everything free for the community to use.

If you or your organisation would like to support AMYBO, sponsor an event, or fund a specific piece of open research, please [get in touch through our contact form](https://contact.andeye.com/?source=amybo.org&subject=Funding%20AMYBO&cb=Grant%20or%20donation&cb=Sponsoring%20an%20event&cb=Funding%20a%20piece%20of%20open%20research&cb=Something%20else).

Right now you can help by [sponsoring lunch or dinner at our 13 November get-together](/events/2026-11-13-london/#food).
