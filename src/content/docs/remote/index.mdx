Some of our open source [electroPioreactors](/projects/electropioreactor/) can be controlled from anywhere: you see the bioreactor's own control screen in your browser, with live video from its camera, and can change its settings as your experiment needs.

**How it works**

1. Apply below and confirm your email address.
2. We assign you to an experiment, and email you its link.
3. Open the link and sign in with the same email address: Cloudflare, which guards the bioreactor for us, emails you a one-time code. There is no password to remember, and only the people assigned to that experiment can sign in.

Access lasts until the end of your course. The bioreactors are real hardware with live cultures, so access is by application only, and we may say no or limit numbers.
