AMYBO is all about letting people grow their own nutritious, delicious and affordable protein.

We are a diverse, non-profit, open source community interested in unlocking the secrets of protein fermentation. Our aim is that anyone will be able to produce tasty, nutritious protein at a lower financial cost, and a much lower environmental cost, than by raising animals to eat.

## How we work

- **In the open.** Designs, experiments and results are shared so that anyone can build on them.
- **Affordable tools.** We develop low-cost open hardware such as the [electroPioreactor](/projects/electropioreactor/), [PioFlo](/projects/pioflo/) and [OpenValve](/projects/other-hardware/openvalve/).
- **Community first.** Biotechnologists, chefs, coders, creatives, engineers, makers, microbiologists, nutritionists and many others all have a part to play.
