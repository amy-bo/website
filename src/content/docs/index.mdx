## What AMYBO is

AMYBO is a not-for-profit, open source community with one aim: that anyone, anywhere, should be able to grow their own nutritious, delicious and affordable protein. Our bet is on hydrogen-oxidising bacteria (HOB for short). Give them hydrogen, oxygen, carbon dioxide and a few salts and they make protein. The three gases can all be made on a benchtop from water, electricity and a SodaStream cylinder, and the salts come in a jar.

A few companies are already scaling this up, behind patents. We want to do it in the open, at a scale a university lab, a school or a keen amateur can afford, and to publish every design, protocol and result as we go.

## We need your help

We need biotechnologists, chefs, coders, creatives, engineers, enthusiasts, environmentalists, makers, medics, microbiologists, nutritionists, others.

- **Join the conversation** on the [forum](https://forum.amybo.org/). Questions are welcome, and so are results that didn't work.
- **Build something** from our [GitHub](https://github.com/amy-bo). Every design and every page of this site can be improved with a pull request; see [how to contribute](/collaborate/contribute/).
- **Follow along** on [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/@AMYBO), or [drop us a line](https://contact.andeye.com/?source=amybo.org&subject=Message%20from%20amybo.org&cb=Volunteering&cb=Running%20an%20experiment&cb=Building%20an%20electroPioreactor&cb=Funding%20or%20sponsorship&cb=Press%20or%20speaking&cb=Something%20else).
