## What AMYBO is

AMYBO is a non-profit, open source community working out how people can grow their own nutritious, delicious and affordable protein. Our focus is hydrogen-oxidising bacteria: microbes that build protein from hydrogen, oxygen and carbon dioxide, which an electro-bioreactor can make from water and a gas cylinder. Everything we design, test and learn is published for anyone to use.

## We need your help

We need biotechnologists, chefs, coders, creatives, engineers, enthusiasts, environmentalists, makers, medics, microbiologists, nutritionists, others.

- **Join the conversation** on the [forum](https://forum.amybo.org/), where experiments, questions and results are shared as they happen.
- **Contribute** on [GitHub](https://github.com/amy-bo): every page of this site and every design in our repositories can be improved by pull request. See [how to contribute](/collaborate/contribute/).
- **Follow along** on [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/@AMYBO), or [get in touch](https://contact.andeye.com/?source=amybo.org&subject=Message%20from%20amybo.org&cb=Volunteering&cb=Running%20an%20experiment&cb=Building%20an%20electroPioreactor&cb=Funding%20or%20sponsorship&cb=Press%20or%20speaking&cb=Something%20else).
