Ran an experiment? We'd love to share it. A negative result is worth as much to us as a positive one, and we publish both.

1. **Post it on the [forum](https://forum.amybo.org/)** with what you did, what you used and what you found. Photos and the raw data help enormously.
2. **Or add a page to this site** through a pull request on [GitHub](https://github.com/amy-bo/website): see the [contribution guidelines](/collaborate/contribute/).

Please follow our [disclaimer](/collaborate/safety-and-legal/disclaimer/), and never eat anything produced in an experiment.
