Ran an experiment? We would love to share it.

1. **Post it on the [forum](https://forum.amybo.org/)** with what you did, what you used and what you found, including the results that did not work.
2. **Or add a page to this site** through a pull request on [GitHub](https://github.com/amy-bo/website): see the [contribution guidelines](/docs/contribution-guidelines/).

Please follow our [disclaimer](/docs/legal/disclaimer/) and never eat anything produced in an experiment.
