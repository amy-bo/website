Experiments run and shared by the AMYBO community:

- [HOB enrichment](/docs/experiments/hob-enrichment/): enrichment culture of hydrogen-oxidising bacteria.
- [Spirulina batch](/docs/experiments/spirulina-batch/): batch cultivation of Spirulina.
- [Control testing](/docs/experiments/control-testing/): comparing results between Pioreactors.
- [CO2 sparging](/docs/experiments/co2-sparging/): a simple CO2 sparging protocol.
- [Anodes](/docs/experiments/electrolysis/): optimising the electroPioreactor's anodes.

See [all experiments](/docs/experiments/), or [submit your own](/experiments/submit/).
