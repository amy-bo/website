Experiments run and shared by the AMYBO community:

- [HOB enrichment](/projects/past/experiments/hob-enrichment/): enrichment culture of hydrogen-oxidising bacteria.
- [Spirulina batch](/projects/past/experiments/spirulina-batch/): batch cultivation of Spirulina.
- [Control testing](/projects/past/experiments/control-testing/): comparing results between Pioreactors.
- [CO₂ sparging](/projects/past/experiments/co2-sparging/): a simple CO₂ sparging protocol.
- [Anodes](/projects/past/experiments/electrolysis/): optimising the electroPioreactor's anodes.

Current results from the Aseptic electroPioreactor work with Imperial and Edinburgh are on [GitHub](https://github.com/amy-bo/electroPioreactor/tree/main/AsepticElectroPioreactor/CARMA_PumpPriming/Results). See [all earlier experiments](/projects/past/experiments/), or [submit your own](/experiments/submit/).
