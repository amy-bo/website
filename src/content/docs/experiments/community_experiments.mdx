Experiments run and written up by the community, oldest first. Some worked, some didn't, and the write-ups say which.

- [Control testing](/projects/past/experiments/control-testing/): do two Pioreactors with the same yeast give the same curve? A good first experiment for anyone with two units.
- [CO₂ sparging](/projects/past/experiments/co2-sparging/): the simplest possible sparging rig, from a SodaStream cylinder, by Gerrit Niezen.
- [Anodes](/projects/past/experiments/electrolysis/): optimising the electroPioreactor's anodes.
- [Spirulina batch](/projects/past/experiments/spirulina-batch/): batch cultivation of Spirulina, which is a good way to learn a Pioreactor before you try anything harder.
- [HOB enrichment](/projects/past/experiments/hob-enrichment/): the first enrichment culture of hydrogen-oxidising bacteria, on video.

Current results from the Aseptic electroPioreactor work with Imperial and Edinburgh are on [GitHub](https://github.com/amy-bo/electroPioreactor/tree/main/AsepticElectroPioreactor/CARMA_PumpPriming/Results) and the [forum](https://forum.amybo.org/t/trial-experiment-on-heterotrophic-electrolysis/163). See [all earlier experiments](/projects/past/experiments/), or [submit your own](/experiments/submit/).
