## Open hardware we develop

- [electroPioreactor](/projects/electropioreactor/): a Pioreactor that makes its own hydrogen and oxygen, with build guides, designs and the plugin on [GitHub](https://github.com/amy-bo/electroPioreactor).
- [PioFlo](/projects/pioflo/): affordable bioreactors at any scale, by turning a Pioreactor into a flow-through cell.
- [OpenValve](/projects/other-hardware/openvalve/): an affordable open source pinch valve.
- [Everything else](/projects/other-hardware/): the CO₂ gas train, holders and small parts.
