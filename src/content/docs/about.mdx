<p class="strapline"><span data-term>Sustainable Protein</span> <span data-term>for <span class="brand-green">All</span></span></p>

- **Sustainable Protein:** protein made without the land, water and emissions that livestock needs. We are working on hydrogen-oxidising bacteria (HOB): microbes that turn hydrogen, oxygen and carbon dioxide into protein, so with water and electricity they make protein from air.
- **For All:** not-for-profit and open source, so the designs are free and anyone, anywhere can make protein locally.

</Mission>

## Mission statement

<Mission>

**AMYBO exists to <span data-term>end the climate crisis</span>, <span data-term>world hunger</span> and <span data-term>poverty</span> by making <span data-term>delicious</span>, <span data-term>wholesome</span>, <span data-term>sustainable protein</span> <span data-term>universally accessible</span>.**

- **Ending the climate crisis:** livestock produces 12–17% of global greenhouse gas emissions ([FAO, 2023](https://openknowledge.fao.org/server/api/core/bitstreams/a06a30d3-6e9d-4e9c-b4b7-29a6cc307208/content); [Twine, 2021](https://doi.org/10.3390/su13116276)). Protein made from air and renewable electricity needs a fraction of that.
- **Ending world hunger:** livestock uses 83% of the world's farmland but supplies only 18% of its calories ([Poore & Nemecek, 2018](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.aaq0216)). Protein made from air needs no farmland and can be made anywhere, independent of weather, soil and season.
- **Ending poverty:** affordable, open designs, so protein can be made, owned and sold locally.
- **Delicious:** it has to be, or nobody will switch. People who have tasted the HOB proteins made so far, from two different bacteria, describe them as mild to almost tasteless ([Green Queen](https://www.greenqueen.com.hk/solein-protein-thin-air-first-tasting-solar-foods/) and [Sifted](https://sifted.eu/articles/solar-foods-protein-solein) on Solein; [Gigazine](https://gigazine.net/gsc_news/en/20191123-air-protein) on Air Protein), which leaves the flavour to the cook. That is why we want chefs involved.
- **Wholesome:** Solein, made from one HOB strain, has as much protein as pea protein isolate and all the essential amino acids adults need, though lab digestion tests rate its digestibility as moderate ([Caliskan et al., 2026](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.foodres.2026.118467)). It has been an approved food in Singapore since 2022 ([Singapore Food Agency](https://www.sfa.gov.sg/docs/default-source/regulatory-standards-frameworks-and-guidelines/2026-07-sfa-list-of-approved-novel-foods.pdf)) and is sold in the US; its EU and UK novel food applications are still being assessed.
- **Sustainable protein:** powered by solar panels, HOB can make over ten times as much protein per hectare as any staple crop ([Leger et al., 2021](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.2015025118)), using about a tenth of the water of soya ([Sillman et al., 2019](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.gfs.2019.09.007)). Electricity is the biggest factor in their carbon footprint, so it needs to be renewable ([Järviö et al., 2021](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.scitotenv.2021.145764)).
- **Universally accessible:** AMYBO is a not-for-profit, open source community. The designs are free and the kit is kept cheap enough to build, own and run locally.

</Mission>

## How we work

Everything goes on [GitHub](https://github.com/amy-bo) and the [forum](https://forum.amybo.org/): the designs, the protocols, the results and the mistakes, so that anyone can build on them. We think open source is also the best protection against a good idea being bought and shelved. And we try to keep the kit cheap, because if it costs more than a small lab or a keen amateur can afford, only well-funded labs will use it. That is why the [electroPioreactor](/projects/electropioreactor/), [PioFlo](/projects/pioflo/) and [OpenValve](/projects/other-hardware/openvalve/) exist.

AMYBO is community-led. Biotechnologists, chefs, coders, creatives, engineers, makers, microbiologists, nutritionists and plenty of people who are none of those things all have a part to play. It is currently a not-for-profit trading name of andeye Ltd, so that it can sign contracts and be paid grants; the name and the logo are placeholders for the community to fill in, as the [brand page](/collaborate/brand/) explains.

## Where we are

Imperial College London and the University of Edinburgh are using our kit under the [CARMA Hub project](/projects/carma-hub/), and Edinburgh has the first AEP 0.2 kits. Students built the first units themselves. The medium, the plugin and the designs are all public. What we don't yet have is a strain we can call our own, and that is the next search: sampling rivers and fens and seeing what grows. If you'd like to help with that, [get involved](/collaborate/volunteer/).
