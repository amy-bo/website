### Lab tours

See the Aseptic electroPioreactors running in Imperial's new labs. The tours are **optional**: they run before the talks, so they suit early arrivals, while anyone arriving later or who has seen the labs already can simply join us at 12:00.

Tours have limited places, so **book a tour when you register**. If a tour is full you can join its waiting list, and we will email you if a place comes up. You can book or change a tour right up to its start, while places remain, using the link in your emails.

## Join remotely

Can't make it to London? The talks will be streamed on **Google Meet**. Choose "remotely" when you register and we will email you the link. The lab tours and networking are in person only.

## Food

Attendance is free. Unless a sponsor comes forward, everyone will need to pop out and buy their own meals:

- **Lunch:** The Works, on the ground floor of the Sir Michael Uren Hub on campus, serves hot food with vegan, vegetarian and gluten-free options.
- **Dinner:** we plan to head to The Broadcaster, 89 Wood Lane, opposite Wood Lane station.
- **Fallback:** Westfield London, next to the campus, has plenty of places to eat.
