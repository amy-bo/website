Join us for a day of lab tours, talks and discussion about open source bioreactors and sustainable protein at Imperial's new labs in White City, and their local pub. It's free (buy your own meals, etc.), and if you can't get to London the talks and some discussion will be live via Google Meet.

AMYBO is a non-profit community working towards Sustainable Protein for All, using hydrogen-oxidising bacteria that make protein from air, water and electricity. Through the [CARMA Hub project](/projects/carma-hub/), Imperial College London and the University of Edinburgh have been working with us to develop an aseptic version of our open-source electroPioreactor. This event is the soft launch of our second prototype [Aseptic electroPioreactor AEP0.2](/projects/electropioreactor/aep/). Come and see it running and hear what we've learned.

## Food

Attendance is free, so unless a sponsor comes forward, please buy or bring your own meals:

- **Snacks and soft drinks:** provided through the day. Soft drinks sponsored by [LabCrafter](https://labcrafter.co.uk/).
- **Lunch:** The Works, on the ground floor of the Sir Michael Uren Hub on campus, serves hot food with vegan, vegetarian and gluten-free options.
- **Dinner:** tick "Dinner" when you register. We have booked a table from 17:30 at [The Hawk's Nest](https://the-hawks-nest.co.uk), a relaxed pizza and cocktail bar under the railway arches at Shepherd's Bush Market (Goldhawk Road, W12 8DF), about 20 minutes' walk or two stops on the Hammersmith & City line from Wood Lane to Goldhawk Road. They currently have a £14.50 Margherita pizza + Margarita cocktail offer on. Pay for your own on the night. Friends can register for just the dinner.
