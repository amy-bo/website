### Lab tours

See the Aseptic electroPioreactors running in Imperial's new labs, and meet the people running them.

Each tour lasts 45 minutes. Closed shoes are needed in the labs, and visitor sign-in details come with your joining instructions.

Each tour has 10 places, so book your space now. If a tour is full you can join its waiting list and we will email you if a place comes up.

## Join remotely

Can't make it to London? The talks will be streamed on Google Meet and recorded. Choose "remotely" when you register and we'll email you the link. The lab tours and the pub are in person only, sorry.

## Food

Attendance is free, so unless a sponsor comes forward, please buy or bring your own meals:

- **Lunch:** The Works, on the ground floor of the Sir Michael Uren Hub on campus, serves hot food with vegan, vegetarian and gluten-free options.
- **Dinner:** tick "Dinner" when you register, and we will try to book a private space at The Broadcaster, 89 Wood Lane, opposite Wood Lane station - pay £45 on the day, but be aware that we will need to pay a £250 deposit, so please let us know ASAP if you can't make it.
- **Fallback:** Westfield London, next to the campus, has plenty of places to eat, but we may struggle to get a large table.
- **Snacks and soft drinks:** provided through the day. Soft drinks sponsored by [LabCrafter](https://labcrafter.co.uk/).
