### Lab tours

See the Aseptic electroPioreactors running in Imperial's new labs, and meet the people running them. The tours are **optional**: they run before the talks, so they suit early arrivals, and anyone arriving later, or who has seen the labs already, can simply join us at 12:00.

Each tour lasts 45 minutes. If you book the 10:30 tour, please arrive by 10:20. Closed shoes are needed in the labs, and visitor sign-in details come with your joining instructions.

Each tour has 10 places, so **book a tour when you register**. If a tour is full you can join its waiting list, and we will email you if a place comes up. You can book or change a tour right up to its start, while places remain, using the link in your emails.

## Join remotely

Can't make it to London? The talks will be streamed on **Google Meet** and recorded. Choose "remotely" when you register and we'll email you the link. The lab tours and the pub are in person only, sorry.

## Food

Attendance is free, so unless a sponsor comes forward, please buy or bring your own meals:

- **Lunch:** The Works, on the ground floor of the Sir Michael Uren Hub on campus, serves hot food with vegan, vegetarian and gluten-free options.
- **Dinner:** tick "Dinner" when you register, or later with the link in your emails. We will try to book The Broadcaster, 89 Wood Lane, opposite Wood Lane station, for everyone who has signed up by 12 noon on Friday 6 November.
- **Fallback:** Westfield London, next to the campus, has plenty of places to eat.
- **Snacks and soft drinks:** provided through the day. Soft drinks sponsored by [LabCrafter](https://labcrafter.co.uk/).
- **Coffee:** cold coffee (unless someone wants to [sponsor](https://contact.andeye.com/?source=amybo.org&subject=Sponsoring%20a%20coffee%20delivery%20at%20the%20AMYBO%20get-together%2C%2013%20November%202026&message=I%20would%20like%20to%20sponsor%20a%20coffee%20delivery%20at%20the%20AMYBO%20get-together%20on%2013%20November%202026.) a delivery).

## Getting there

- **Tube:** Wood Lane station (Hammersmith & City and Circle lines) is a few minutes' walk away, and White City station (Central line) is also close.
- **Room:** 516, at 84 Wood Lane. Entrance and visitor sign-in details will be in your joining instructions before the day. If you have access needs, tell us on the form: we pass them to Imperial only where that is needed to arrange access.
