The mixed-culture electroPioreactor is where the project started: a standard Pioreactor vial with an electrode pair through the cap and a CO₂ sparging line, without the aseptic sealing of the [AEP](/projects/electropioreactor/aep/). It is cheaper and simpler, and it suits enrichment cultures from environmental samples, where the aim is to find hydrogen-oxidising bacteria rather than keep a known strain pure. It is currently used by MSc students at the University of Edinburgh.
