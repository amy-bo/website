The Aseptic electroPioreactor keeps a defined culture defined: a sealed, parametric vial cap, 0.2 μm hydrophobic vent filters on the gas ports and luer-lock connections for feed and sampling, on top of the electrolysis and CO₂ sparging of every electroPioreactor. It is designed to be safe first, then accurate and repeatable, then automated, then good value.

## Versions

- **AEP 0.1 and 0.1.1** (2025): six units assembled and used for training at Imperial and Edinburgh in November 2025. [Assembly instructions](https://github.com/amy-bo/electroPioreactor/blob/main/AsepticElectroPioreactor/CARMA_PumpPriming/Assembly/AEP0.1.1_Assembly.md).
- **AEP 0.2** (2026): revised vial cap, electrode top stop, GL45 media bottles and an offline card-install route for the plugin. Kits shipped to both universities in September 2026. The step-by-step guide, with video and drawings for each step, is being published at [docs.electropioreactor.org/AEP](https://docs.electropioreactor.org/AEP) using [Docs&I](https://github.com/andeyePro/docsandeye).
