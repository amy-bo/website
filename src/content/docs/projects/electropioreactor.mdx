The electroPioreactor is a [Pioreactor](https://pioreactor.com) adapted to grow hydrogen-oxidising bacteria (HOB). Electrodes in the culture make hydrogen and oxygen from the medium as they are needed, and a relay-driven solenoid sparges CO₂, so the bacteria can fix carbon without any stored hydrogen. Everything is designed, documented and version-controlled in the open at [amy-bo/electroPioreactor](https://github.com/amy-bo/electroPioreactor).

There are two variants:

- **Aseptic electroPioreactor (AEP).** Sealed vial cap, 0.2 μm vent filters and luer-lock ports for defined single-strain research. Six AEP 0.1 units were assembled and used for training at Imperial and Edinburgh in November 2025; the AEP 0.2 kits shipped in September 2026. This is the focus of the [CARMA Hub Pump Priming](https://carmahub.co.uk/about-us/pump-priming-projects/) project led by Sonja Billerbeck (Imperial College London) and Chris French (University of Edinburgh), with AMYBO as design and training partner and [LabCrafter](https://labcrafter.co.uk) as supplier.
- **Mixed-culture electroPioreactor (MEP).** The original, lower-cost version for enrichment cultures and teaching, currently used by MSc students at Edinburgh.

## Run it: the Pioreactor plugin

The [electroPioreactor plugin](https://github.com/amy-bo/electroPioreactor/tree/main/AEP-Plugin) adds one background job to the Pioreactor web interface: it drives electrolysis at a set power, sparges CO₂ on a schedule, pauses electrolysis during each sparge, and pauses optical-density readings until the bubbles have cleared. All four settings can be changed live.

- [Install it from the SD card or over SSH](https://github.com/amy-bo/electroPioreactor/tree/main/AEP-Plugin#installation), including the [card bundles](https://github.com/amy-bo/electroPioreactor/releases) for units with no network access.
- The plugin is also published on its own at [amy-bo/pioreactor-electroPioreactor-plugin](https://github.com/amy-bo/pioreactor-electroPioreactor-plugin).
- Our changes to Pioreactor's own software and OS images go upstream from [amy-bo/pioreactor](https://github.com/amy-bo/pioreactor) and [amy-bo/CustoPiZer](https://github.com/amy-bo/CustoPiZer).
