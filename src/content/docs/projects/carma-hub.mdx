**Affordable Aseptic Electro-Bioreactor for Reproducible Hydrogen Oxidising Bacteria (HOB) Research** is funded by [CARMA Hub Pump Priming](https://carmahub.co.uk/about-us/pump-priming-projects/). It is developing and testing the [Aseptic electroPioreactor](/projects/electropioreactor/aep/) so that HOB research can be done reproducibly on affordable, open equipment.

## Who

- [Dr Sonja Billerbeck](https://profiles.imperial.ac.uk/s.billerbeck), Imperial College London, Bezos Centre for Sustainable Protein: principal investigator.
- [Prof. Chris French](https://biology.ed.ac.uk/chris-french-laboratory), University of Edinburgh: principal investigator and collaborator.
- AMYBO: design and training partner.
- [LabCrafter](https://labcrafter.co.uk): supplier of the kits.

## Progress

- November 2025: six AEP 0.1 units assembled and used for training at Imperial and Edinburgh.
- 2026: the AEP 0.2 designed from what the AEP 0.1 taught us; kits delivered to both universities in September 2026, with the build guide to follow at [docs.electropioreactor.org/AEP](https://docs.electropioreactor.org/AEP).
- Results, meeting notes and training records are in the [repository](https://github.com/amy-bo/electroPioreactor/tree/main/AsepticElectroPioreactor/CARMA_PumpPriming).

See the AEP 0.2 units running at the [AMYBO get-together on 13 November 2026](/events/2026-11-13-london/) at Imperial's White City labs.
