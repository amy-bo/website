In an electroPioreactor the growth medium is also the electrolyte, so it must feed the bacteria without evolving chlorine, ammonia or other harmful products at the electrodes. Our formulation takes hydrogen and oxygen from electrolysis and carbon dioxide from a SodaStream cylinder, the mesonutrients of Sydow et al. (2017), and a two-bottle micronutrient solution of our own.
