We keep our reading in the open too. The bibliography is a BibTeX file in the [repository's Literature folder](https://github.com/amy-bo/electroPioreactor/tree/main/Literature), rendered by BibBase, so anyone can add a paper with a pull request.

- The Zotero group library is what our [journal club](https://forum.amybo.org/c/journal-club) reads from. Join the forum and come along.
- The [growth medium](/projects/media/) page has its own review of the media papers, including the one where nobody at the lab that wrote it could remember why they'd used hydrochloric rather than sulphuric acid.
- The [proteins](/background/proteins/) pages are the background reading on why hydrogen-oxidising bacteria, and what else we considered.
- If you can get hold of a paper we can't, or have written one we should know about, [tell us](https://contact.andeye.com/?source=amybo.org&subject=A%20paper%20for%20the%20AMYBO%20bibliography&cb=A%20paper%20to%20add&cb=Something%20else).
