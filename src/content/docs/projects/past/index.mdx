These pages are from 2023 and 2024, when AMYBO was a handful of people working out which protein to make, what to make it in, and whether the whole idea would work on a kitchen table. They explain how we got to the electroPioreactor, and we've left them as they were written, typos and all.

- [Bioreactors: why we chose the Pioreactor](/projects/past/equipment/bioreactors/), with the rest of the [equipment notes](/projects/past/equipment/), including a pressure cooker standing in for an autoclave.
- [First experiments](/projects/past/experiments/): CO₂ sparging, control testing between two Pioreactors, electrode optimisation, HOB enrichment and a Spirulina batch, videos included.
- [electroPioreactor v0.02](/projects/past/electropioreactor-v0-02/), the 2024 mixed-culture design with its full bill of materials, and the [2024 electroPioreactor page](/projects/past/electropioreactor-2024/).
- [Kickstarting for good](/projects/past/kickstarting-for-good/), an idea for funding open hardware that we never got round to.
- Background from the same period: the [2023 overview](/background/overview/), the [2023 plan](/background/plan-2023/) and the [proteins](/background/proteins/) pages.
