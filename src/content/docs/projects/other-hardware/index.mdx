Besides the electroPioreactor itself, the project has produced and studied several smaller pieces of open hardware.

- [**OpenValve**](/projects/other-hardware/openvalve/): a 3D-printable solenoid pinch valve. Designed by Harvey Mudd College's clinic team ([valve team files](https://github.com/amy-bo/electroPioreactor/tree/main/PastResearch/HMC-Clinic/ValveTeamFiles)) and now being revived by LabCrafter.
- [**CO₂ transfer**](https://github.com/amy-bo/electroPioreactor/tree/main/Components/CO2%20transfer): regulators, needle valves and venting solenoids for the gas train, with what we learned about each.
- [**Anode work**](https://github.com/amy-bo/electroPioreactor/tree/main/PastResearch/HMC-Clinic/AnodeTeamFiles) by Harvey Mudd College's anode team, and the [clinic poster](https://github.com/amy-bo/electroPioreactor/blob/main/PastResearch/HMC-Clinic/AMYBO%20Poster%20Presentation.pptx.pdf).
- [**CO₂ sparger backflow**](https://github.com/amy-bo/electroPioreactor/tree/main/PastResearch/Brown-HarrisLab): diagnosis and a revised-component fix by Eli Silver at Brown University's Harris Lab.
- [**Holders and small parts**](https://github.com/amy-bo/electroPioreactor/tree/main/Components): the GL45 bottle holder, pinch slider and electrode top stop.
- [Equipment notes from 2023](/projects/past/equipment/): autoclaves, microscopes, DNA sequencing and the bioreactor survey that led us to the Pioreactor.
